## 1. Why does `num_attention_heads_for_vit` exist?

Self-attention in the Vision Transformer enables image patches to attend to other patches globally across the 2D canvas. Multi-head attention splits the channel dimension into parallel representation subspaces, allowing simultaneous tracking of spatial edges, object boundaries, textures, and semantic relationships.

```text
Hidden State [1408] ──► Split into H = num_attention_heads_for_vit (16 heads)
                            │
                            ▼
                    Each head dim = 1408 / 16 = 88
                            │
                            ▼
                    Parallel Spatial Self-Attention
```

`num_attention_heads_for_vit` configures the count of attention heads in each ViT layer.

---

## 2. Options and Defaults

| Value | Head Count | Head Dimension (with hidden_size=1408) |
|---|---|---|
| `16` (Default) | 16 heads | $1408 / 16 = 88$ |
| `12` | 12 heads | ViT-Base ($768 / 12 = 64$) |

---

## 3. Interactions

- **`hidden_size_for_vit`**: Must be evenly divisible by `num_attention_heads_for_vit`.
- **`remat_policy_for_vit`**: Rematerialization operates over the attention maps generated by these heads.

---

### One-line intuition
> **`num_attention_heads_for_vit` sets the number of parallel attention heads in the Vision Transformer encoder.**
